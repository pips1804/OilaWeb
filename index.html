Product Overview

Why Choose Oila?

Oila is an eco-friendly and affordable dishwashing liquid developed for B2B use. It delivers strong grease-cutting performance while supporting environmentally responsible cleaning practices.

Designed for businesses that require effective cleaning at a manageable cost, its formulation is perfect for high-use kitchen environments.

Key Benefits

  • ✓ Strong grease-cutting power
  • ✓ Affordable for daily business use
  • ✓ Eco-friendly formulation
  • ✓ Consistent quality for food businesses

Ingredients List

  • Used cooking oil (filtered)
  • Activated Charcoal
  • Potassium hydroxide (KOH)
  • Water (for KOH solution)
  • SLES (Sodium Laureth Sulfate)
  • Coco Betaine
  • Sodium Silicate
  • Salt (dissolved in water)
  • Sodium Benzoate
  • Potassium Sorbate
  • Lemon Fragrance Oil
  • Yellow Color
  • Extra water
Directions to Use
1

Dispense

Press the pump to apply dishwashing liquid directly onto the sponge.

2

Squeeze

Squeeze the sponge to activate the soap and create foam.

3

Scrub

Scrub the plate using the soapy sponge to remove food and grease.

4

Rinse

Rinse with water. Enjoy streak-free, squeaky clean dishes that smell of fresh citrus.
